About Danny Bullock

Owner & Broker, Bluestem Real Estate and Land

Bluestem Real Estate and Land was built on a simple idea: real estate decisions deserve the same scrutiny an investor would apply to any other asset — not just a listing price and a sign in the yard.

Danny Bullock brings 30 years of professional sales experience to every transaction, paired with his own personal background investing in real estate. That combination shapes how Bluestem approaches residential sales, land and ranch property, and commercial brokerage across southcentral Kansas.

Why It Matters

Most agents are trained to list and show property. Danny evaluates property the way an investor would — what it’s actually worth, what it could be worth, and what risks or opportunities a buyer or seller should know about before signing anything.

That applies whether the deal is a family’s first home, 160 acres of row crop ground, or a small commercial building. The questions are the same: What is this actually worth? What does the data say? What would a serious buyer need to know?
